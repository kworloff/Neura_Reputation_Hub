# Neura Reputation Hub - Project Description

## What your application does

Neura Reputation Hub is a decentralized reputation management platform that calculates reputation scores for EVM wallets based on their on-chain activity (transactions, balance, wallet age, and social interactions). Users can mint reputation tokens (REP) based on their calculated score, transfer these tokens to other users with personalized messages, and use them for voting in DAO governance. All reputation transfers are displayed in a public activity feed, creating a transparent trust system for Web3 addresses.

## What problem it solves

The platform solves the lack of trust and identity verification in Web3 ecosystems by providing a transparent, on-chain reputation system. It addresses the difficulty of establishing credibility for wallet addresses, enables reputation-based DAO governance instead of simple token voting, and helps build trust networks in decentralized communities. The system creates accountability for wallet addresses, helps identify reliable participants, and provides a way for new users to build reputation through activity and community recognition.

Most importantly, Neura Reputation Hub democratizes Web3 participation by giving power to regular users rather than large capital holders. Unlike traditional token-based systems where voting power and influence are determined by wealth, reputation is earned through genuine activity, community engagement, and long-term participation. This means that active community members with modest balances can have meaningful influence in DAO governance, while new users can build reputation through consistent participation rather than large financial investments. The platform levels the playing field by rewarding contribution and trustworthiness over capital, making Web3 more accessible and fair for everyone.
